# Example Purchasing Follow-up Policy

FICTIONAL DEMO POLICY. Applies only to the example workspace. Version 1, effective 2026-10-01. These are sample company choices, not industry requirements.

## Overdue goods

An issued purchase order line with outstanding quantity is overdue when its current confirmed delivery date has passed. Cancelled and closed lines are excluded. Keep the original promised date separately for supplier performance reporting.

If the delivery date is missing, request a confirmed date. Do not label the line overdue solely because the date is missing.

## Follow-up and escalation

Prepare a supplier follow-up for an overdue line. A buyer reviews the message before it is sent.

Escalate to the purchasing manager when the supplier has not responded for two business days after a follow-up, or when a linked customer delivery within three business days is demonstrably affected. A link and a current stock check are required before claiming a customer impact.

If a response is already due within an agreed window, show that commitment instead of preparing a duplicate reminder.

## Replacement products

Do not promise a replacement automatically. Obtain technical compatibility approval and buyer approval before proposing a substitute to the customer.
