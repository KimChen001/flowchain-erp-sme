# Example Supplier Invoice Review Policy

FICTIONAL DEMO POLICY. Applies only to the example workspace. Version 1, effective 2026-10-01. Not accounting or legal guidance.

## Price differences

A unit-price difference between the invoice and the agreed PO price requires buyer review before approval. This example policy sets no automatic tolerance.

Compare the same line and unit of measure, and show tax separately. A difference between the whole PO total and a partial invoice total does not by itself prove a price variance.

## Invoice before goods

An invoice may be recorded before the goods arrive. Mark the receipt link as pending; do not create a fictitious receipt or claim the goods have arrived. Matching and approval follow when the necessary evidence is available.

## Payment

Only an authorized finance user may confirm payment through the business workflow. A question or a knowledge answer is not a payment instruction.
